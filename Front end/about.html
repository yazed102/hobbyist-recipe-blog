About Hobbyist

A recipe blog for people who actually enjoy cooking.

Cooking does not have to be perfect to be good.

Hobbyist started with a simple idea: cooking does not have to be perfect to be good.

Browse recipes, find something you want to try, change it when you feel like it, and keep the ones that become part of your routine. It is a small collection, built around everyday food rather than complicated rules.

Whether you are making breakfast on a slow morning, preparing lunch between classes, or trying something new for dinner, Hobbyist is here to give you recipes that are simple, useful, and worth making at home.
