BREAKFAST

Pancakes

Fluffy homemade pancakes that are perfect for a simple and delicious breakfast.

PREP TIME 10 min
COOK TIME 10 min
SERVINGS 4

Recipe by Hobbyist

A simple homemade favorite

This recipe is easy to prepare and can be customized with your favorite toppings.

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

HOW TO MAKE IT

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. Add the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  4. Pour the batter into the pan.
  5. Flip the pancake when bubbles appear.
  6. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.

MAIN DISH

Homemade Pizza

A crispy homemade pizza topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and fresh toppings.

PREP TIME 15 min
COOK TIME 20 min
SERVINGS 4

Recipe by Hobbyist

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh tomatoes
  • Fresh basil
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

HOW TO MAKE IT

Instructions

  1. Prepare and roll out the pizza dough.
  2. Spread tomato sauce over the dough.
  3. Add mozzarella cheese and toppings.
  4. Bake until the crust is golden.
  5. Add fresh basil after baking.
  6. Slice and serve warm.

RICE

Fried Rice

Quick and flavorful fried rice packed with vegetables and simple seasonings.

PREP TIME 10 min
COOK TIME 15 min
SERVINGS 4

Recipe by Hobbyist

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Salt and pepper

HOW TO MAKE IT

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a large pan.
  2. Add garlic and vegetables.
  3. Add the eggs and scramble them.
  4. Add the cooked rice.
  5. Add soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
  6. Cook for a few minutes and serve.

PASTA

Tomato Basil Pasta

A simple pasta dish made with fresh tomatoes, basil, garlic, and olive oil.

PREP TIME 10 min
COOK TIME 15 min
SERVINGS 4

Recipe by Hobbyist

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • 250g pasta
  • 2 fresh tomatoes
  • Fresh basil
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Parmesan cheese

HOW TO MAKE IT

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. Heat olive oil and add the garlic.
  3. Add the chopped tomatoes.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Add the cooked pasta and fresh basil.
  6. Serve with Parmesan cheese.
