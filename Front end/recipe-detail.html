Tomato Basil Pasta

By Hobbyist Kitchen   ·   25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 250 g pasta
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Fresh basil
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper

How to make it

  1. Cook the pasta until al dente.
  2. Warm the olive oil and gently cook the garlic.
  3. Add the tomatoes and cook until softened.
  4. Toss with the pasta and fresh basil.
  5. Season, serve, and make it yours.

Pancakes

By Sarah   ·   15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

How to make it

  1. Mix the flour, sugar and baking powder.
  2. Add the milk and eggs.
  3. Mix until the batter is smooth.
  4. Cook the pancakes in a hot pan.
  5. Serve with your favourite topping.

Breakfast Eggs

By Omar   ·   10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Fresh herbs

How to make it

  1. Heat the butter in a pan.
  2. Crack the eggs into the pan.

  4. Cook until the eggs are ready.
  5. Add fresh herbs and serve.

Classic Pizza

By Alex   ·   25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 200 g mozzarella cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Fresh basil

How to make it

  1. Prepare the dough and let it rest for 20 minutes.
  2. Roll the dough into a thin circle.
  3. Spread tomato sauce over the dough.
  4. Add mozzarella and basil.
  5. Bake until the crust is golden and the cheese melts.

Fried Rice

By Sarah   ·   20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

How to make it

  1. Heat the oil in a pan.
  2. Cook the egg and break it into small pieces.
  3. Add the vegetables and cook for a few minutes.
  4. Add the cooked rice.
  5. Add soy sauce and mix everything together.

Lemon Cake

By Mario   ·   60 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • Fresh lemon juice

How to make it

  1. Mix the butter and sugar together.
  2. Add the eggs and lemon juice.
  3. Add the flour and mix well.
  4. Pour the mixture into a cake pan.
  5. Bake until the cake is golden and cooked through.
