BREAKFAST

Pancakes

Fluffy homemade pancakes that are perfect for a simple and delicious breakfast.

PREP TIME 10 min
COOK TIME 10 min
SERVINGS 4

Recipe by Hobbyist

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

HOW TO MAKE IT

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
  2. Add the milk, egg, and melted butter. Mix until the ingredients are combined.
  3. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  4. Pour some batter into the pan and cook until bubbles appear on the surface.
  5. Flip the pancake and cook the other side until golden brown.
  6. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.
